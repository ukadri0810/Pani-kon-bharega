📖 How to Play Each Game

💧 Water Duty Wheel
  1. Add everyone to the roster and tap SPIN FOR WATER DUTY.
  2. The chosen player gets assigned as today's Jal Mantri along with a funny rhyming shayari.
  3. No excuses allowed—the selected person takes the bottle and refills it!
⚡ Rapid Panic (5s / 10s / 15s)
  1. Pick a player for the hot seat and select timer difficulty (5s, 10s, or 15s).
  2. Tap START ROUND. A 3-second preparation countdown lets you read the prompt before the timer fires!
  3. The player must shout 3 distinct answers before the buzzer hits 0. Tap Passed to give them +1 Point.
🕵️ The Office Imposter
  1. Pass the device one by one. Each player taps the card to view their role privately.
  2. Everyone sees the Secret Word, EXCEPT 1 randomly chosen Imposter (who only sees the broad category).
  3. Go in a circle: each player says one single clue word related to the secret topic.
  4. After clues, the room debates and votes on who is bluffing!
🤐 Taboo Clues
  1. One player (the guesser) turns their back so they cannot see the screen.
  2. The rest of the team describes the top cyan word without saying any of the 4 red forbidden words!
  3. No rhyming or spelling allowed. If guessed correctly, award the team a point.
🎭 Emoji Mystery
  1. The screen displays emojis representing a Bollywood movie, brand, or office habit.
  2. The first person in the room to shout the correct decoded answer gets the point!
  3. Tap REVEAL to verify who was right.
