📖 BreakRoom Game Directory

💧 Water Duty Wheel
  1. Spin the wheel to select today's designated bottle filler.
  2. The chosen player gets a clean, funny Hinglish shayari taunt on screen!
🔗 The Missing Link (Untimed)
  1. Look at all 4 clue cards on screen.
  2. Brainstorm and debate as a group: what single theme, word, or fact connects all four?
  3. Tap REVEAL LINK once everyone has made their final guesses!
🤔 Odd One Out (Debate Battle)
  1. There is no single right answer! Every player chooses one item.
  2. Each person gives their funniest or smartest argument why their pick is the odd one out.
  3. The room votes on who gave the best explanation!
🧐 Fact or Fiction
  1. Read the bizarre real-world fact on screen.
  2. Vote whether you think it's 100% FACT or a clever FICTION lie before clicking to reveal the verdict!
🔮 20 Questions (The Oracle)
  1. One player taps the Oracle box secretly to check the mystery target.
  2. The rest of the group asks strategic Yes/No questions to deduce the target before running out of 20 questions!
⚖️ Dilemma Arena (Would You Rather?)
  1. Two funny, quirky scenarios appear on screen.
  2. Everyone picks a side and tries to convince the other side why their choice is superior!
🧠 Mind Reader (Consensus Match)
  1. Read the open category prompt. On the count of 3, everyone shouts their answer simultaneously!
  2. The goal is to see who naturally thinks alike and matches answers with the most teammates.
🕵️ The Office Imposter
  1. Pass the device secretly. Everyone sees the secret word except 1 imposter!
  2. Take turns saying a 1-word clue. Debate and vote out who is bluffing!
🤐 Taboo & 🎭 Emoji Mystery
  1. Taboo: Describe the top word without saying any of the 4 red forbidden words!
  2. Emoji: First person to shout the decoded movie, brand, or phrase wins.
