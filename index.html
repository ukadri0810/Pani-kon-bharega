📖 HOW TO PLAY: THE IMPOSTER ▼
  1. Pass the Device: Each player secretly taps the card to check their word and hides it again.
  2. The Mystery: Everyone sees the real secret word, EXCEPT 1 randomly assigned Imposter!
  3. The Clues: Go in a circle; everyone says *one single clue word* related to the secret topic.
  4. The Vote: Debate and point at who you think is bluffing. Catch the imposter before they guess the word!

📖 HOW TO PLAY: TABOO CLUES ▼
  1. The Guesser: One player turns their back to the screen so they cannot see the card.
  2. The Clue Givers: The rest of the team describes the top main word to the guesser.
  3. Forbidden Words: You CANNOT say any of the 4 red forbidden words or spellings/rhymes!
  4. Goal: See if the guesser can guess the exact word within 30 seconds.
